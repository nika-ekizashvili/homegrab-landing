Privacy Policy კონფიდენციალურობის პოლიტიკა

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Your privacy is central to our design philosophy. HomeGrab functions as a "local-first" application. This means we do not maintain user registration databases, backend application servers, or listing databases. Your data remains in your custody on your local device.

2. Data Processing & OAuth Integration

To enable synchronization with your Google Sheets, HomeGrab requests access through Google's OAuth 2.0 verification protocol. Here is how your data is handled:

  • OAuth Permissions Requested: HomeGrab requests access to write to your Google Sheets (limited to spreadsheets you create or choose to open with the application, specifically to log your listing sync operations).
  • Token Storage: Google OAuth Access and Refresh Tokens are securely stored within Chrome's local storage engine (`chrome.storage.local`). They are encrypted/protected by your operating system's browser sandbox environment.
  • Server-Free Pipeline: When you successfully publish a listing, the extension sends data directly from your local browser to the Google Sheets API. At no point does this data transit or store on any intermediate servers belonging to HomeGrab.

3. Information Collected Locally

The extension temporarily accesses and reads variables in active browser tabs to execute its core operations:

  • Scraped Listing Information: Property title, description, price, contact numbers, area (m²), images, layout characteristics (bedrooms, floors, utilities) from MyHome.ge and SS.ge. This is held in temporary memory and is immediately wiped when the tab is closed or a new scrape starts.
  • Configuration Preferences: User settings (such as Agent Name, Phone Surcharges, and rounding margins) are saved in local storage to prevent needing re-entry. You can reset these at any time in the popup control board.

4. Third-Party Access & Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your information to any third parties. Your automated requests to MyHome.ge and SS.ge are made directly from your computer, carrying your local browser configuration and cookies, which behave as if you were manually inputting the details.

5. Security Measures

Since all operations run locally on your device, the security of your stored configuration depends on your device security and Google Account protection. We recommend enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) on your Google accounts linked to Google Sheets.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You can revoke HomeGrab's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Security settings under "Third-party apps with account access." Uninstalling the Chrome extension will instantly delete all local configurations, cache, and token data from your local browser storage.
